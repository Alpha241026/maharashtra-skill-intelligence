4. ASSUMPTIONS & METHODOLOGY NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Work-mode qualifiers ('Hybrid -', 'Remote -', 'Work from home -', 'Temp. WFH -') are stripped to evaluate the true geographical intent of the posting.
2. Suburbs, industrial estates, and talukas in Maharashtra (e.g. Chakan, Hinjewadi, Waluj, Airoli, Vashi, Vasai, Panvel, Butibori) are mapped to their recognized district/city administrative center.
3. Multi-location postings containing both Maharashtra and other Indian tech hubs (e.g. 'Hyderabad, Pune, Bengaluru') are classified as 'maharashtra_multi_location'. In 'district_or_city', all identified Maharashtra locations are preserved (e.g. 'Pune') without inventing an arbitrary single primary district.
4. Pure non-Maharashtra postings that slipped through broad keyword filters (e.g. 'Kawardha', 'Durg', 'Gandhidham') are strictly tagged as 'non_maharashtra' with empty 'district_or_city'.
5. Generic search placeholders ('Anywhere India', 'MIDC' without city) are tagged as 'uncertain'.
6. Original 'location' column is preserved 100% untouched. No rows deleted.
